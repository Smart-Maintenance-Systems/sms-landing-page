## COPY

**Eyebrow:** NO PANIC, BY DESIGN

**Headline:** Built the same way.

**Lead:**
We didn't just tell you not to panic — we built the whole product around it. SMS Workboat is designed to be
honest and proportionate: the same calm, plain-spoken approach the Code actually asks for.

**Four design truths (cards — claim bold + plain explanation):**

1. **It shows what you've done — it never grades you.**
   No pass, no fail, no "compliance score." Just a clear view of what you've recorded and what's still open.
   Nova, the assistant built in, will never tell you you're compliant — that's the surveyor's call, not an app's.

2. **Every "done" is one a surveyor could trust.**
   When something needs a look, it says so — plainly. It would rather show you an honest amber than a comforting
   green that isn't earned. Nothing here is dressed up to look finished when it isn't.

3. **Start small. Build it up.**
   A one-boat operation's SMS is meant to be small — so setup is four plain areas, not a wall of forms. Do what
   you can today, and come back and add the rest. It's built to be *started*, not finished in one sitting.

4. **Your surveyor gets exactly what they sample.**
   When the examination comes, you hand over your self-assessment and the evidence behind it — in their language,
   the way they actually check it. No last-minute scramble, no separate folder to build.

**Close (pull-quote):**
An honest message deserves an honest tool. This is one.
